MoodMail settings

MoodMail uses Google's Gemini free tier to rewrite your text. Get a free key

MoodMail starts with this model. If it's busy or at its free limit, MoodMail tries the other free models for you.

Use MoodMail in text boxes on any website, like job applications and contact forms. It always skips passwords, payment details, ID numbers, contact details and search boxes. You can turn it off for one site from the toolbar menu.

Your privacy

On the free tier, Google can use what you send to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Google says not to send sensitive, confidential or personal information. Read Google's terms

  • Only the text you typed in that box goes to Google, and only after you pick a mood.
  • Be careful with forms that ask about your health, money or legal matters.
  • Your signature and any quoted email thread stay out of the request.
  • Your API key is stored on this device and is never synced or shown to web pages.
  • MoodMail never sends an email or submits a form for you.
