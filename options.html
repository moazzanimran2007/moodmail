MoodMail settings

MoodMail uses Google's Gemini free tier to rewrite your text. Get a free key

MoodMail starts with this model. If it's busy or at its free limit, MoodMail tries the other free models for you.

Your instructions and notes

Saved on this device and added to your requests. Nothing here is synced.

Notes help MoodMail get names and facts right. They're sent with moods and requests, not with "Just fix the grammar".

Like your résumé or a product sheet. Text files are read on this device. Gemini reads PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Excel files once, when you add them. Up to 5 documents, 10 MB each.

    Also sends the email you're replying to, or a form's question and the text on its page. You can switch this on the card each time too.

    Use MoodMail in text boxes on any website, like job applications and contact forms. It always skips passwords, payment details, ID numbers, contact details and search boxes. You can turn it off for one site from the toolbar menu.

    Your privacy

    On the free tier, Google can use what you send to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Google says not to send sensitive, confidential or personal information. Read Google's terms

    • Nothing goes to Google until you pick a mood or send a request.
    • What you typed goes, with your notes and, if page context is on, the email you're replying to or the form's question and page text. "Just fix the grammar" sends only what you typed.
    • Be careful with forms that ask about your health, money or legal matters.
    • Documents are kept on this device. PDF and Office files go to Google once to be read, and the text of the documents you switch on goes with moods and requests. Leave out private details like your address or ID numbers.
    • Your signature is never sent, and a quoted email thread is only ever used as context, never rewritten.
    • Your API key is stored on this device and is never synced or shown to web pages.
    • MoodMail never sends an email or submits a form for you.
